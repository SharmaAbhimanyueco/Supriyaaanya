    How reliable is it?

    Method: two clear March Landsat 8 images per year were merged into one cloud-free picture, sorted by K-means into 10 groups (trained on all pixels from the three years, one model for every year), and the groups were named and merged into four land types. The analysis was run in Python; the course asked for QGIS, and the same steps can be repeated there.

    Data: Landsat 8 Collection 2 (USGS/NASA) via Microsoft Planetary Computer. Accuracy check: Esri World Imagery and ESA WorldCover. Project by Supriya Mahshere and Aanya Upadhyay, November 2026.
